BIOGRAPHY

A life observed through place, people and change.

Mehari Teshome was born in Ethiopia in the mid-1980s and was raised in Addis Ababa, around Merkato, one of East Africa’s major open-air marketplaces.

Growing up there gave him a close view of different communities, values and ways of living. The portfolio describes him as a self-taught artist who developed his practice through observing human interaction with nature, society in the digital era, and the value of women in life.

His work asks how society structures and operates itself within the conditions of the 21st century.

ARTISTIC PRACTICE

Geometry, texture and layered thought.

01

Materials

The portfolio describes a mixed-media process that often begins with an acrylic base, followed by oil paint, textured papers and fabrics. Painting knives and brushes are used to build heavily impastoed surfaces. Charcoal drawings and sketches explore tone, light and shadow.

02

Merkato

Merkato is a central source of visual inspiration: movement, noise, commodities, display arrangements, human interaction and the layered complexity of the marketplace feed his use of colour, form and composition.

03

The frame

His work examines identity, contrast, social needs and human connection, including the feeling of being framed by urban life, mass media and transmitted ideas.

04

Cyber era

The artist statement considers the rapid spread of innovations and ideas and asks what can be lost when African societies are pulled away from their organic expressions, indigenous wisdom and cultural mechanisms of adaptation.

ARTIST STATEMENT

The cyber era and its other side.

The portfolio frames the work as an investigation of the gaps created by a rapidly changing information environment — the “vacuums” between new systems and the values, memories and expressions that existed before them.
